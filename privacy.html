Privacy

Privacy Policy

Your reflections are the most private thing you'll ever type. This page explains what happens to the little we actually hold.

Draft — not yet final. This is a working draft written in plain language so you can read it. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer, and a Canadian privacy lawyer must review it (for PIPEDA and BC PIPA) before Mend launches. Don't rely on it as a final legal document. Last updated 26 July 2026.

In one line: right now, the only thing Mend collects is the email address you give the waitlist. Nothing else — no logs, no journals, no tracking that follows you around the web.

What we collect today

At this stage Mend is a waitlist and a set of information pages. The only personal information collected is:

  • Your email address, if you choose to join the waitlist. It's handled by our form provider (Formspree) purely so we can email you once when spots open.

That's the entire list. We do not ask for your name, your children's information, or anything about what happens in your home.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent, or trade your email to anyone. Ever.
  • We don't use your information to train any AI model.
  • We don't run advertising trackers, third-party ad pixels, or cross-site profiling.
  • We don't add you to a newsletter or a drip sequence. Joining the waitlist gets you one email when there's something real to tell you, and nothing else.

The app (when it exists)

The Mend app isn't built yet, so it collects nothing from you today. When it does exist, the intention described across this site is that your logs — what set you off, and how bad it got — are encrypted at rest, never sold, never used to train anything, and exportable and deletable by you. That paragraph will be rewritten into a precise, lawyer-reviewed policy before the app ships. Until then, treat it as a statement of intent, not a guarantee.

How long we keep it

We keep your waitlist email only until the waitlist has served its purpose (we've launched and contacted you) or until you ask us to remove it, whichever comes first. If you join and then decide you'd rather not be on the list, we delete your address.

Deleting your information

Email hello@mend.example from the address you signed up with and ask to be removed. We'll delete it and confirm. No hoops.

Where it's processed

Your email is processed by Formspree, our form provider, which may store it on servers outside Canada. Before launch, this section will name every processor we use and where each one holds data.

Children

Mend is for parents and other caregivers — adults. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes

When this becomes a final, lawyer-reviewed policy, the draft banner above will be removed and the date updated. Any material change will be reflected here.

Contact

Questions about privacy go to one person: hello@mend.example. Mend is based in North Vancouver, BC, Canada.
