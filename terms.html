What Mend is

Mend is a self-guided tool for reflection and repair, made for parents and caregivers. Today it is a waitlist and a set of information pages; the app is in development and is not yet available.

What Mend is not

This is the important part, and it doesn’t change once the app ships:

  • Mend is not therapy and not a therapist. It does not diagnose, treat, or counsel, and it is not a substitute for care from a licensed professional.
  • Mend is not a crisis or emergency service. If someone is in danger, use the emergency numbers in the footer of this page.
  • Mend does not give medical or legal advice. Nothing here is a professional opinion about you, your child, or your situation.

Please don’t misuse the site — no attempts to break it, scrape it, overload it, or use it to harm anyone. The waitlist is for your own genuine sign-up; don’t add other people’s addresses.
